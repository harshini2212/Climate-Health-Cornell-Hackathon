## 01:38 — missingness: not built, and it is not the bottleneck
Measured before building, on the real 10k panel and the four busiest hazard days: hiding a seeded 20% of **five** fields and marginalising per draw moves `find_out` from 0.055% to 0.057% of veteran-days. `p_epistemic_share = Var(p)/(p_mean(1-p_mean))` is exactly `p(1-p)·Var(eta)`, so clearing 0.4 at the `p>=0.10` gate needs `Var(eta)≈4.4`; one hidden *binary* field adds at most `mu²/4`, and the biggest coefficient in the model (`theta_outage_x_cold_chain`, mu=0.90) gives 0.20. The rung-0 share distribution is mean 0.049, **p99 0.271, max 0.415** — `tiers.EPISTEMIC_CUT = 0.4` sits above its 99.9th percentile. Handed to the api lane: **0.30 → 2.43%**, 0.25 → 6.9%, 0.20 → 17.6%; an absolute cut will need re-tuning when rung 1 tightens the posterior, so consider top-N%-for-that-date instead. Missingness is still worth building for its own claim (hidden veterans' mean share is +8.9% over observed, 0.0535 vs 0.0492) — it is being done in another lane, so I did not touch it. Note `deployment_era` is read by **no** design term, so hiding it cannot widen any interval.

## 02:01 — missingness, and why it was not what was blocking the find-out tier
*(Written in parallel with 01:38 above, which reached the same diagnosis from the same arithmetic — including that `deployment_era` is read by no design term. This entry is what got built. The one place it disagrees: 01:38 hands the api lane a lower `EPISTEMIC_CUT` (0.30 → 2.43%), and that number is real but it is paid for out of act-now, which needs the same share **below** the cut — at 0.30 act-now goes 1.369% → 0.341%, at 0.25 → 0.004%. The gap rule below reaches the same 2.43% with act-now untouched, and it is a threshold crossing rather than a quantile of a variance, so it does not need re-tuning when rung 1 tightens the posterior.)*
`cohort/missingness.py` hides a seeded 20% each of `home_ac`, `floor` and `deployment_era` on independent streams (~49% of veterans keep a complete record), writing nullable `<col>_observed` siblings; the truth stays in the cohort for `simulate.py` and `score_prior.py` now reads only the observed copy, marginalising over what it cannot see by drawing the unknown from its population rate once per posterior draw. `test_scoring_cannot_read_the_truth` flips the hidden truth and asserts no score moves. **That alone moved `find_out_share` from 0.226% to 0.231%, not to 2%.** The prompt's diagnosis was wrong: `p_epistemic_share` at rung 0 is dominated by the prior spread every veteran shares, and across 6,000,000 rows it tops out at 0.426 against the 0.4 cut, so hiding *100%* of AC and floor changes nothing material. Lowering the cut is not available either — act-now needs the same share *below* it, so at 0.25 find-out reaches 3.1% and act-now collapses from 1.37% to 0.004%. The two rules were fighting over one threshold.

So the tier now reads a different column. `scores.parquet` gains nullable `p_gap_lo`/`p_gap_hi` — the `p_mean` this veteran would be reported with if the fields the VA does not hold turned out to be their least- and most-risky values, averaged over the same draws so all three are comparable (taking the ends at the mean coefficients puts `p_mean` outside its own range at these probabilities). Find-out fires when that range straddles a line the team acts on, 0.10 or 0.25 — both already in `tiers.py`, no new constant. **find-out 0.226% → 9.197% of actions (2.43% of veteran-days), act-now untouched at 16,424 veteran-days, `harm_averted_k40` unchanged at 16.31, `ece_max` 0.0075 → 0.0076, fairness still 0 flagged (max FNR ratio 1.035 → 1.048).** `make check` green; baseline recorded as "missingness + gap-aware find-out".

Contract changes, so **re-run `make cohort && make score`**: cohort gains three `_observed` columns, scores gains `p_gap_lo`/`p_gap_hi`, `api.NeedScore` carries both. SPEC §3.1, §3.4 and §7.5 updated to match. Cost is `make score` 17.3s → 46.8s (one extra design build per candidate value per week); `make demo` and `make clean-clone` are unaffected, they read cached parquets and fixtures.

Three for other lanes. `deployment_era` is hidden but moves no score — no design term reads it, it reaches the model only via `pact_presumptive`, which is recorded separately and not hidden; the scorer works this out from `Expr.meta.root_names()` rather than a hardcoded list, so a term that starts reading it is marginalised over for free. `scripts/make_ui_fixtures.py` had its own copy of the tier explanation that had already drifted from the API's; it now imports `api.main.why_tier`, so the offline fixture path and the live demo say the same sentence.
