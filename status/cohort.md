## 01:38 — missingness: not built, and it is not the bottleneck
Measured before building, on the real 10k panel and the four busiest hazard days: hiding a seeded 20% of **five** fields and marginalising per draw moves `find_out` from 0.055% to 0.057% of veteran-days. `p_epistemic_share = Var(p)/(p_mean(1-p_mean))` is exactly `p(1-p)·Var(eta)`, so clearing 0.4 at the `p>=0.10` gate needs `Var(eta)≈4.4`; one hidden *binary* field adds at most `mu²/4`, and the biggest coefficient in the model (`theta_outage_x_cold_chain`, mu=0.90) gives 0.20. The rung-0 share distribution is mean 0.049, **p99 0.271, max 0.415** — `tiers.EPISTEMIC_CUT = 0.4` sits above its 99.9th percentile. Handed to the api lane: **0.30 → 2.43%**, 0.25 → 6.9%, 0.20 → 17.6%; an absolute cut will need re-tuning when rung 1 tightens the posterior, so consider top-N%-for-that-date instead. Missingness is still worth building for its own claim (hidden veterans' mean share is +8.9% over observed, 0.0535 vs 0.0492) — it is being done in another lane, so I did not touch it. Note `deployment_era` is read by **no** design term, so hiding it cannot widen any interval.
